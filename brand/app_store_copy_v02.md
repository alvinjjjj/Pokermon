```
HKCardColl is Hong Kong's first platform built for Pokémon TCG collectors. Pull live prices for Japanese and English cards in one place, with automatic HKD conversion. Three daily chores collapsed into one app.

— Four pillars —

01 · Pricing
Live data from TCGplayer (EN, USD), Cardmarket (EN, EUR), and Japanese retailers (JP, JPY). Auto-converted to HKD. PSA 10 and BGS 9.5 graded premiums tracked alongside raw.

02 · Portfolio
Log every card with buy price, language, and grade. Total value and 7 / 30 / 90-day trend charts update automatically. Retire the spreadsheet.

03 · Trade
A local Hong Kong P2P marketplace. Individual sellers list up to 10 cards. Certified merchants — verified by Business Registration — list up to 100, with a dedicated shop page and a verification badge. Certified pricing feeds the HK Market Price Index.

04 · Community
Social feed, unboxing posts, follow other collectors, direct messages. Shops find buyers; buyers find shops they can trust.

— Two-tier merchant system —

Tier 1 · Individual seller — self-registered, instant, max 10 listings.
Tier 2 · Certified merchant — BR document reviewed manually (1–3 business days), verified badge, max 100 listings, pricing counts toward the HK index.

— Why HKCardColl —

· Hong Kong's first synchronized JP + EN pricing
· Local lens, global price: HKD as the base, original JP / EN prices shown alongside
· Hong Kong-built; currency support for HKD, USD, JPY, CNY
· Four languages: Traditional Chinese (HK), Simplified Chinese, English, Japanese

— Who it's for —

· Hong Kong collectors holding both JP and EN cards
· Collections valued from low five to seven figures HKD
· Anyone currently tracking on spreadsheets and trading on Facebook or WhatsApp

— Who it's not for —

· Casual booster-pack openers without a tracking habit
· General second-hand toy auctions

— About us —

Built by POTO Creative Tech Limited (BR 80363844). Registered in Hong Kong, May 2026. The team are Hong Kong collectors themselves.
```
